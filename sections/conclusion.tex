\section{Conclusion and Future Work}

ArchSync deterministically links declared contracts to source-derived TypeScript service graphs. On co-developed datasets, graph, classification, evidence-location, replay, and incremental/full-scan outputs match regression oracles. The detector corpus exposes earlier analyzer errors and verifies their correction through provenance tracking.

These results do not establish general accuracy, comparative advantage, or governance effectiveness. Data co-evolved with the implementation; accepted external comparisons, a common label mapping, and an independent real-world holdout are absent. The analyzer never rewrites the model; evolution requires human review. External accuracy, performance, and developer-value claims require auditable evidence from an independent multi-repository holdout, capability-matched external comparison, and real pull-request pilot.
