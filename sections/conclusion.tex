\section{Conclusion and Future Work}

ArchSync maps a declared contract to a source-derived TypeScript graph; controlled graph, classification, evidence, replay, and scan outputs matched regression oracles, and its detector corpus exposed analyzer errors. The study demonstrates controlled feasibility, not external accuracy or advantage: the data are synthetic and co-evolved, with no external baseline or independent holdout. Violations block; evolution requires human review. Future work requires an external holdout, matched baseline, and pull-request pilot. Such evidence can reduce, but not eliminate, external-validity threats. Only after these gates produce auditable evidence may the paper claim external accuracy, performance, or developer value.
