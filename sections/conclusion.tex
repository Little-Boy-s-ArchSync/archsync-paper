\section{Conclusion and Future Work}

ArchSync deterministically links declared contracts and source-derived TypeScript service graphs. Controlled outputs match the declared regression oracles, and provenance tracking corrects earlier detector errors on the targeted corpus.

They do not establish general accuracy, comparative advantage, or governance effectiveness. Data co-evolved with the implementation; accepted external comparison and common label mapping are absent, and no independent real-world holdout exists. The analyzer never rewrites the model; evolution needs human review. Future gates are an independent multi-repository holdout, capability-matched external comparison, and real pull-request pilot. Only after those gates produce auditable evidence can external accuracy, performance, or developer value be claimed.
