\section{Conclusion and Future Work}

ArchSync deterministically links declared contracts and source-derived TypeScript service graphs. Controlled outputs match the declared regression oracles, and provenance tracking corrects earlier detector errors on the targeted corpus.

They do not establish general accuracy, comparative advantage, or governance effectiveness. Data co-evolved with the implementation; no external comparison has been accepted into these results, and no independent real-world holdout exists. The analyzer never rewrites the model; evolution needs human review. A prepared, module-only, nonblind two-author D3 study is a prospective exploratory comparison, not a substitute for an independent holdout. Future gates include its separately reported execution, an independently curated multi-repository evaluation, and a real pull-request pilot. Only after those gates produce auditable evidence can the corresponding comparative, external-accuracy, performance, or developer-value claim be made.
