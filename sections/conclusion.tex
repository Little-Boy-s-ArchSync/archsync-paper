\section{Conclusion and Future Work}

ArchSync deterministically links declared contracts and source-derived TypeScript service graphs. Controlled outputs match the declared regression oracles, and provenance tracking corrects earlier detector errors on the targeted corpus.

They do not establish general accuracy, comparative advantage, or governance effectiveness. No external comparison is reported and no independent real-world holdout exists. The prepared module-only, nonblind D3 study is exploratory, not independent validation. The analyzer never rewrites the model; evolution needs human review. Future gates are D3 execution, an independently curated holdout, and a real pull-request pilot. Only after those gates produce auditable evidence can corresponding external-accuracy, performance, or developer-value claims be made.
